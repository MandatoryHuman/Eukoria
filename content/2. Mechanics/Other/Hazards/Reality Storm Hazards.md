This page contains sample PF2e hazards for the regional manifestations described in [[Reality Storms]]. They are environmental hazards rather than creatures. A GM can use them as written, combine several into a larger storm, or adjust their levels to fit the party.

## Absence Shockwave - Hazard 3

**Uncommon, Complex, Environmental, Magical**

**Stealth** DC 20 (trained)

A silent wave of Absence passes through the area, briefly erasing a person's connection to their own history.

**Description** A 30-foot line of distorted air, usually visible only when it passes through dust, rain, or loose debris.

**Disable** DC 20 Occultism or Survival (trained) to mark and avoid the wave's path; DC 23 Religion or Thievery (expert) to reinforce a physical or emotional Anchor. Two total successes are required. A creature carrying an activated [[2. Mechanics/Items/Anchors/index|Anchor]] gains a +2 circumstance bonus to these checks.

**AC** 19; **Fort** +9, **Ref** +7

**Hardness** 5; **HP** 30 (BT 15); **Immunities** critical hits, object immunities

**Routine** (1 action) The shockwave moves 30 feet in a straight line. It can pass through creatures and objects, but not through an area protected by a successful Disable check. Each creature it passes through attempts a DC 20 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and is temporarily immune to this hazard for 1 minute.

**Success** The creature takes 2d6 mental damage.

**Failure** The creature takes 2d6 mental damage and is stupefied 1 for 1 minute. The creature forgets one minor, nonessential detail about the last 24 hours.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 4d6 mental damage, is stupefied 2 for 1 minute, and loses one specific useful memory until it receives appropriate magical treatment or the GM decides the memory returns. This should remove a specific fact, not the player's control of the character.

**Reset** The shockwave dissipates after 1 minute, then reforms in a nearby area after 10 minutes.

## Tidal Inversion - Hazard 7

**Complex, Environmental, Magical, Water**

**Stealth** DC 26 (expert)

A section of the God-Scar Strait briefly forgets which direction is down. Water rises into the sky, then falls as jagged sheets of glass-hard spray.

**Description** A 60-foot burst of unstable water centered on a point in the sea or on a ship's deck.

**Disable** DC 26 Nature or Sailing Lore (expert) to steer around the inversion; DC 28 Arcana or Crafting to stabilize the affected vessel or structure. Three total successes are required. A character adjacent to a ship's wheel, anchor chain, or similar control point can attempt one check as part of the Disable action.

**Trigger** A creature, vessel, or unattended object enters the area.

**Effect** The water twists upward. Every creature and vessel in the area attempts a DC 25 Reflex save. A vessel uses its captain's Sailing Lore modifier or the pilot's Reflex save, whichever is higher.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is pushed 10 feet toward the edge of the area.

**Failure** The target takes 4d6 slashing damage and is pushed 20 feet toward the edge. A creature that ends this movement in the water is pulled 10 feet downward.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target takes 8d6 slashing damage and is [[off-guard|off-guard]] until the end of its next turn. A vessel also takes 20 damage.

**Routine** (1 action) On its initiative, the inversion shifts 20 feet in a random direction and repeats its Effect. The hazard cannot shift into an area protected by a successful Disable check.

**Reset** The inversion ends after 3 rounds, but may reform elsewhere in the same body of water after 1 hour.

## Passion Bloom - Hazard 5

**Complex, Environmental, Magical, Mental, Polymorph**

**Stealth** DC 23 (expert)

A kaleidoscopic fog from Heretic's Wake forces the body to express an emotion as sudden physical change.

**Description** A 20-foot burst of brightly colored fog that obscures sight beyond 10 feet. The fog is difficult terrain for creatures that can see it.

**Disable** DC 23 Arcana, Nature, or Occultism (expert) to redirect the fog; DC 25 Medicine to help an affected creature resist the transformation. Two total successes are required.

**Trigger** A creature begins its turn in the fog or casts a spell while inside it.

**Effect** The triggering creature attempts a DC 23 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature grows a harmless, temporary mutation and is dazzled until the start of its next turn.

**Failure** The creature takes 3d6 mental damage and is clumsy 1 for 1 minute. It grows a harmless mutation that imposes a -1 circumstance penalty to one type of check chosen by the GM, such as appearing trustworthy or using a precise tool.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is clumsy 2 and stupefied 1 for 1 minute. The mutation also grants a compensating ability, such as a swim Speed, scent, or telepathy with a range of 10 feet, chosen by the GM. The ability lasts for 1 minute.

**Routine** (1 action) The fog expands by 5 feet in every direction.

**Reset** The fog disperses after 5 rounds without a creature casting a spell inside it.

## Entropic Squall - Hazard 11

**Complex, Environmental, Magical**

**Stealth** DC 32 (master)

An ash-gray cloud accelerates the natural erosion of everything within it. Metal flakes, masonry crumbles, and living bodies strain under their own weight.

**Description** A 40-foot burst of gray ash. The area is concealed, and creatures inside cannot benefit from item bonuses to AC or saving throws from non-magical equipment.

**Disable** DC 32 Crafting or Survival (master) to identify a path through the squall; DC 34 Arcana or Religion (master) to anchor the storm to a fixed location. Four total successes are required.

**Trigger** A creature or object enters the area, or a round begins while a creature remains inside.

**Effect** The triggering creature and each unattended object in the area are exposed to erosion. A creature attempts a DC 30 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes 4d6 bludgeoning damage.

**Failure** The creature takes 8d6 bludgeoning damage and is enfeebled 1 for 1 minute. One held or worn non-magical item becomes broken after the damage is resolved.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 12d6 bludgeoning damage, is enfeebled 2 for 1 minute, and one held or worn item becomes broken. A magical item instead becomes temporarily weakened and cannot be activated for 1 minute.

**Routine** (2 actions) The squall moves 15 feet in a random direction, then uses its Effect. It ignores difficult terrain and can pass through openings as small as 1 inch.

**Reset** The squall dissipates after 10 rounds, leaving behind a 20-foot-radius dead-magic zone for 1 hour.

## Scaling Reality Storms

For a lower-level version, reduce the hazard's DCs by 2, reduce its damage by one die, and reduce its HP by 10. For a higher-level version, increase the DCs by 2, increase damage by one die, and increase HP by 20. The GM should avoid stacking memory loss, item destruction, and persistent conditions without giving the party clear warning signs and opportunities to retreat.
