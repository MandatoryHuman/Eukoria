_Uncommon, Archetype, Dedication_
\[Prerequisites::Trained in Nature, Occultism, or Religion]
You gain the ability to parse objective truths from the chaos of battle. When you select this feat, choose your Truthseeker Path, which determines the source of your insight and the trait of your archetype abilities:

- **Providential Path(Divine):** You read the cosmic ledgers of morality and fate. **Prerequisites** Trained in Religion.
- **Verdant Path (Primal):** You read the physical flaws and survival instincts etched into nature. **Prerequisites** Trained in Nature.
- **Esoteric Path (Occult):** You read psychic echoes, spiritual attachments, and minds. **Prerequisites** Trained in Occultism.
  You gain the **Uncover Truth** reaction.

## Uncover Truth ↩

_Concentrate, Mental_
**Trigger:** One of the following occurs:

- You critically succeed on a Strike against a creature.
- You critically succeed on a Spell Attack against a creature.
- You critically succeed on a Recall Knowledge check against a creature.
- You roll Initiative at the same time as a creature and beat their result by 10 or more.
  **Effect:** You gain 1 Piece of Truth about the triggering creature.

Additionally, you learn how to subtly exploit this foundational knowledge, gaining the following effect as long as you have at least one Piece of Truth concerning a creature.
**1  Piece of Truth** When you cast a spell targeting the creature, you briefly whisper this truth, forcing a -1 status penalty to their saving throws against your spells.

_Special: You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Truthseeker archetype._
