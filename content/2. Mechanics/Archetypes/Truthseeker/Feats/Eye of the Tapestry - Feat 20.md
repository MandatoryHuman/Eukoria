\[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]

_Archetype, Stance_
You open your mind entirely to the flow of metaphysical information, allowing the universe to feed you truths simply by observing the battlefield.

## Focus Eye ◆

_Concentrate, Stance_
You enter a state of heightened investigative awareness. While in this stance, at the end of each of your turns, you automatically gain 1 Piece of Truth about a creature of your choice that you can currently perceive.
