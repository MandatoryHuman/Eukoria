_Archetype_
\[Prerequisites:: [[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]
You know how to burn away a piece of metaphysical knowledge to alter fate, sacrificing long-term leverage for momentary survival or power.

## Burn Truth ↩

_Concentrate_
**Trigger** you are about to roll a Saving Throw against an effect originating from a creature you hold at least 1 [[Pieces of Truth|Piece of Truth]] for.
**Effect** You expend 1 Piece of Truth about the creature. You lose any passive benefits that required that piece of knowledge. You gain a +3 status bonus to your triggering saving throw.
