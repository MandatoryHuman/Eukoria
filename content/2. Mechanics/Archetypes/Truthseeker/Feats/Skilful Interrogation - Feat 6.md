*Archetype*
[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]
You actively carve secrets out of your foes through sheer skill. Add the following triggers to your _Uncover Truth_ reaction:
- You critically succeed on an **Acrobatics** check to **Tumble Through** the creature's space.
- You critically succeed on an **Athletics** check made as part of an **Attack** against the creature.
- You critically succeed on a **Deception** check to **Feint** or **Create a Diversion** against the creature.
- You critically succeed on a **Diplomacy** check to **Make an Impression** on or use Bon Mot against the creature.
- You critically succeed on an **Intimidation** check to **Demoralize** the creature.
- You critically succeed on a **Medicine** check on an ally that was harmed by the creature within the last minute.
- You critically succeed on a **Perception** check to **Sense Motive** against the creature.
- You critically succeed on a **Stealth** check to **Hide** or **Sneak** against the creature’s Perception DC.
- You critically succeed on a **Survival** check to **Track** the creature.
- You critically succeed on a **Thievery** check to **Steal** from the creature.
