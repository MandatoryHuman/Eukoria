_Archetype_
You have a knack for filtering the metaphysical truth out of local gossip and half-whispered legends. When you critically succeed on a check to Gather Information to learn about a specific, named creature, you gain 1 Piece of Truth about them. This only applies to creatures that you do not already have any Pieces of Truth on.
