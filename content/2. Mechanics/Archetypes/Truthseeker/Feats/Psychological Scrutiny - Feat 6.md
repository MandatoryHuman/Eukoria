_Archetype_
\[Prerequisites:: [[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]
You map the contours of a creature's mind through conversation, deception, and intimidation. Add the following to the list of triggers you can Uncover Truth on.
- You Critically succeed on a Deception check to Feint or Create a Diversion.
- You Critically succeed on a Diplomacy check to Bon Mot or Make an Impression.
- You Critically succeed on an Intimidation check to Demoralise.
- You Critically succeed on a Perception check to Sense Motive against them.
