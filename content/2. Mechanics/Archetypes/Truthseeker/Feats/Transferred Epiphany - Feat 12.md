_Archetype_
You understand how the flaws and fears of one creature ripple outward to affect those around them.

## Truth Cascade ◇

_Concentrate_
**Trigger** A creature you hold at least 1 Piece of Truth for is reduced to 0 Hit Points.
**Effect** You witness the collapse of their personal narrative and draw immediate parallels. You may choose one hostile creature within 30 feet of the triggering creature. You immediately may attempt a DC 11 Flat check. On a Success, you gain one Piece of Truth on that creature. On a Critical Success, you instead gain a number of Pieces of Truth equal to the number that you had on the first creature when it died.
