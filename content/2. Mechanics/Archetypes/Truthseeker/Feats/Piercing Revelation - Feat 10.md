*Archetype*
\[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]
You can weaponize a creature's metaphysical flaws to guide your strikes through their defences.

## Strike True ◇
**Trigger** You hit a creature you hold at least 1 Piece of Truth for with a Strike or Spell Attack. **Effect** You expend and forget 1 Piece of Truth about the creature. Your attack ignores an amount of the creature's Resistances equal to half your level (rounded up). Additionally, the attack deals an extra 2d6 precision damage, as you strike a newly uncovered vulnerability.
