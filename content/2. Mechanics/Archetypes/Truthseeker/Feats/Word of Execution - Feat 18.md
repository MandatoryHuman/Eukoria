_Archetype_
\[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]
You possess the ultimate, crushing realisation of a creature's existence, allowing you to sever their connection to life itself.

## Speak Finality ◆◆

_Death, Auditory_
**Requirements** You currently hold 5 Pieces of Truth about the target.
**Effect** You speak the totality of the creature's existence aloud, shattering its soul. You cast _execute_ as a 10th-rank innate spell of your chosen tradition targeting the creature. Once you use this ability against a creature, you immediately lose all Pieces of Truth about them, and you cannot gain Pieces of Truth about that specific creature for 24 hours.
