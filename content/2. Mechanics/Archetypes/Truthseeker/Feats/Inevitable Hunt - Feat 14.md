_Archetype, Primal_
The natural world whispers their exact location to you on the wind.

## Lead Me To My Quarry ◆◆◆

_Concentrate, Primal_
You expend 2 Pieces of Truth about a creature to locate them. For the next hour, you know the rough distance and direction to the creature as long as you are on the same plane of existence. For this duration, you gain a +4 Status Bonus to checks made to Track that creature. Additionally, for the same duration, any time the creature attempts to use a teleportation effect  it must succeed at a Will save against your Spell or Class DC or the teleportation fails and is wasted.
