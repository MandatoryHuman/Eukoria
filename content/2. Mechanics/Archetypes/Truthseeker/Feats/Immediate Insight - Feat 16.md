\[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]

_Archetype_
Your intuition has become razor-sharp, allowing you to glean vital information in the opening milliseconds of an encounter. At the start of your first turn in any combat encounter, you automatically gain 1 Piece of Truth about a single hostile creature you can currently perceive.
