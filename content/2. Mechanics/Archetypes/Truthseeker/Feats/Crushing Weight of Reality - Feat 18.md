_Archetype_
\[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]
To hold the complete metaphysical blueprint of a creature's existence is to hold their soul in a vice. The mere act of perceiving them begins to dismantle their physical form.
**5 Pieces of Truth:** As long as the target creature is within your line of sight, they are Enfeebled 2 and Clumsy 2. This is a passive effect of your overwhelming scrutiny; these conditions cannot be reduced or removed by any means until the creature breaks your line of sight or you lose your Pieces of Truth about them.
