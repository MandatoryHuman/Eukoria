_Archetype_
\[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]]]
The more you understand a creature's structure and soul, the easier it is to unmake them. You gain the following benefits against creatures as you gain more [[Pieces of Truth]] about them.
**2 Pieces of Truth** The target gains Weakness to your attacks and spells equal to half your level (rounded up).
