_Archetype, Occult_, _Revealing_
You command the spirits of those the creature has wronged to plague their senses based on your gathered knowledge.
**3 Pieces of Truth** Invisible echoes constantly badger the target. The creature takes a -2 status penalty to Perception checks, and if it attempts to use a Reaction, it must succeed at a DC 6 flat check or lose the reaction.
