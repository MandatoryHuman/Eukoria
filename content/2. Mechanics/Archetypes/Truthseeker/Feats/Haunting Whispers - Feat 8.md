*Archetype, Occult*
[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]], Esoteric Path]
You command the spirits of those the creature has wronged to plague their senses based on your gathered knowledge. 
**3 Pieces of Truth** Invisible echoes constantly badger the target. The creature takes a -1 status penalty to Perception checks, and if it attempts to use a Reaction, it must succeed at a DC 5 flat check or lose the reaction.
