*Archetype, Divine*
[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]], Providential Path]
You possess enough divine authority over a creature's destiny to deny them succour. 
**3 Pieces of Truth** The target is denied grace. If the creature is the target of a healing effect or attempts to regain Hit Points, you automatically attempt to counteract that effect.
