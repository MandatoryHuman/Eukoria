_Archetype_, _Uncovering_
You map a creature's physical limits and reflexive instincts in the chaotic heat of battle. Add the following to the list of triggers you can Uncover Truth on.

- You Critically succeed on an Acrobatics check to Tumble Through their space.
- You Critically succeed on an Athletics check to Disarm, Grapple, Shove, or Trip them.
- You Critically succeed on a Thievery check to Steal an item they are holding or wearing.
- You Critically succeed on a Perception check to Seek them.
