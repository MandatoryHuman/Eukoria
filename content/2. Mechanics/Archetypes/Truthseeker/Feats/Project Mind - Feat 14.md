\[Prerequisites::[[Truthseeker Dedication - Feat 2|Truthseeker Dedication]], Esoteric Path]

_Archetype, Occult_
You understand a target's mind so thoroughly that you can simply slip inside it.

## Psychic Hijack ◆

_Concentrate, Mental, Occult_
**Requirements** you currently hold at least 3 Pieces of Truth about the target.
**Effect** you tap into the target's senses. Choose a creature within 120 feet. For 1 minute, you can see through their eyes and hear what they hear, gaining the benefits of any special senses they possess. During this time, you have Line of Sight and Line of Effect to anything they do, including themselves, and you can cast spells and use abilities with the Mental trait as though you occupied their space.
