title: Automaton

Automaton are almost exclusively built by the inventors of [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Federal Republic of Ilsyaneas/index|Ilsyaneas]] - though there are of course exceptions. In Ilsyaneas, these artificial forms are used to house the souls of terminally ill citizens. This process does not normally transfer the memories of the citizens. In nations that adhere more strictly to the teachings of the [[1. World Almanac/World/Gods & Divines/Greater Gods/Canonical Gods/index|Canonical Gods]], the process of creating an automaton can be controversial, as it could be considered Relic [[Traditions of Dismagic|Dismagic]] leading most to either stay within Ilsyaneas, or emigrate to areas that follow the [[1. World Almanac/World/Gods & Divines/Greater Gods/Non-Canonical Gods/index|Non-Canonical Gods]], such as [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/Heretic's Wake/index|Heretic's Wake]].
