Nephilim in [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/index|Eukoria]] are most commonly those who are born near to or directly inside of [[Reality Storms]], or the children of other Nephilim. The type of Nephilim a reality storm creates is never entirely predictable, but there does seem to be a correlation - for example a reality storm in [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Daeshin Null-State/index|The Daeshin Null-State]] is more likely to create a Grimspawn while one in [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Kazarn Hegemony/index|The Kazarn Hegemony]] is more likely to create a Battleblooded.
