**Consumable, Magical, Anchor, Fortune**
**Price** 3gp
**Usage** worn or carried; **Bulk** -
**Activate** ◇; **Trigger** you fail a saving throw against an effect caused by a Reality Storm

---

The most basic form of [[2. Mechanics/Items/Anchor/index|Anchor]] that offers limited protection from [[Reality Storms]], though not the creatures that bode within. When activated, you may reroll the triggering saving throw. You must use the new result, even if it is worse.
