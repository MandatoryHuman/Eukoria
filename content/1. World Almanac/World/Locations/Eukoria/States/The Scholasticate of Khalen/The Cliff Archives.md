# Overview

The Cliff-Archives serve as the sheer, vertical capital of [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Scholasticate of Khalen/index|The Scholasticate of Khalen]]. Built directly into the colossal cliff faces overlooking the churning [[God-Scar Strait]], it is a dizzying labyrinth of libraries, scriptoriums, and pulley-elevators. To a traveller arriving by sea, the city looks like a massive honeycomb of pale stone glowing with lamplight against the dark rock. The air is a constant mixture of freezing, salty sea spray and the dry, musty scent of old parchment.

# Geography & Layout

The city has almost no horizontal footprint. It is entirely vertical, consisting of deep caverns carved into the cliff and external wooden scaffolding, bridges, and suspension platforms hanging over the deadly drop to the ocean below. The bottom-most levels are battered by the sea and serve as heavily fortified docks for the salvage fleets, while the highest, driest peaks house the most precious, ancient texts recovered from the deep.

# Government & Law

The city is ruled by the Curator Assembly, a council of the most elite scholars and archaeologists in Khalen. The laws here are obsessively focused on historical preservation. Violence and theft are punished with standard fines or imprisonment, but the destruction of a book, map, or pre-war artefact is considered high treason. Such crimes are punished by permanent exile or forced labour in the deep-sea dredging fleets.

# Districts

- The High Stacks: The uppermost echelons of the city, safe from the damp sea air. This wealthy, quiet district houses the Curator Assembly, the most prestigious academies, and the heavily warded vaults containing recovered pre-war magical theory.
- The Pulley-Wards: The bustling middle tiers where the majority of the population resides. It is a chaotic web of suspended bridges, residential caverns, and public scriptoriums where scribes work day and night to copy degrading, water-logged texts.
- The Delver's Drop: The lowest, most dangerous district at sea level. It is a rugged, industrial zone of docks, diving bells, and taverns, populated almost entirely by the hardened scavengers who brave the reality storms of the Strait.

# Notable Locations

- The Drowned Rat: A rowdy, perpetually damp tavern in the Delver's Drop. It is the primary gathering place for aquatic scavengers returning from expeditions, known for its potent spiced rum and loud, boastful storytelling.
- Salt & Vellum: A premier adventuring shop in the Pulley-Wards. They sell high-level water-breathing potions, pressure-resistant diving gear, and restorative alchemicals designed to preserve rotting paper.
- The Grand Scriptorium: A massive temple dedicated to [[Oclera, God of Knowledge]]. Inside, dozens of clerics sit in meditative trances, using Oclera's hive-mind magic to collectively translate and process fragmented historical texts pulled from the drowned ruins.

# Key NPCs

- [[High Curator Vaelis]]: (Level 15 Elf Scholar) The pragmatic, exhausted head of the Curator Assembly. She views the recovery of history not as an academic pursuit, but as a vital military defence against the amnesia of the world.
- Captain Rurik: (Level 12 Dwarf Fighter/Delver) A gruff, scarred veteran who runs the Abyssal Delvers guild. He has lost an eye and a hand to the mutated beasts of the Strait, replacing the latter with a rusted, pre-war grappling hook.

# Factions & Guilds

- The Curator Assembly: The governing body of scholars and politicians.
- The Abyssal Delvers: The elite, highly respected guild of aquatic scavengers and monster hunters.
- The Silent Order: A secretive, underground group that prays to the memory of [[Syla, Goddess of Records]], operating out of the High Stacks. Though their practices are not technically illegal, they try to avoid the attention of the Church of [[Oclera, God of Knowledge]], as well as the government of Khalen.

# History & Lore

The Cliff-Archives were founded in the aftermath of the [[War of Laws]]. As the cataclysm drowned the coastline when [[Lethos, God of Trickery|Lethos]] assassinated [[Syla, Goddess of Records|Syla]], the surviving scholars of the region fled to these cliffs. They carved the first libraries into the rock by hand to protect what few books they had managed to save. Over the last 116 years, the city expanded downwards towards the water as their mission shifted from mere survival to actively dredging the drowned ruins of their ancestors.
