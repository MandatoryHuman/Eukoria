# Overview

Prismathia is the dazzling, illusory capital of [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Radiant Court of Gethis/index|The Radiant Court of Gethis]]. To a traveller passing through the city gates, it is a breathtaking metropolis of impossible architecture, hovering crystal spires, and eternal twilight festivals. The air always smells of blooming jasmine and sweet wine. However, almost none of it is real. The true city is built on a bleak, rocky wasteland, but it is entirely cloaked in a state-supported, city-wide Occult mirage known as the "Great Masquerade," designed to hide the horrific reality of the apocalypse lurking on their southern borders.

# Geography & Layout

The actual, physical terrain of Prismathia is uneven, scarred, and covered in grey dust blowing in from [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Daeshin Null-State/index|The Daeshin Null-State]]. The illusory layout, however, is a masterpiece of magical urban planning. There are glowing, hard-light bridges that span phantom canals, and the buildings shift their aesthetic styles daily based on the artistic whims of the ruling Duke. Navigating the city requires trusting the illusions; stepping where a phantom bridge appears to be is safe, as telekinetic wards physically support the mirage.

# Government & Law

The city is ruled by an incredibly vain aristocratic oligarchy, overseen by Grand Duke Valerius. The laws here revolve entirely around aesthetics, etiquette, and maintaining the illusion. The greatest crime in Prismathia is acknowledging the truth. Speaking openly about the reality storms, the pirates, or the crumbling state of the world is a punishable offence. Treason and severe crimes are punished not with execution, but by "Dropping the Veil"—having the magical illusions permanently dispelled from the offender's home and person, exposing them to the raw, depressing reality of the wasteland and marking them as a total social outcast.

# Districts

- The Glass-Promenade: The uppermost noble district, boasting the highest concentration of permanent, high-level illusions. The estates here are sprawling, impossible palaces of phantom crystal and light.
- The Painter's Ward: The bohemian district where artisans, lesser nobles, and the Veil-Weavers reside. It is a chaotic, vibrant area where experimental illusions and magical street-art frequently clash.
- The Gloom: The hidden slums located on the city's outskirts, housing the foreign labourers and mercenaries who do the city's physical work. Due to limited magical resources, illusions frequently flicker or fail entirely here, revealing the depressing, rocky dirt beneath the masquerade.

# Notable Locations

- The Mirrored Mask: A high-society lounge where patrons are required to wear illusory disguises. It is a hotbed of political intrigue, where nobles plotting treason can speak freely under the guise of anonymity.
- The Duke's Palette: An opulent, sprawling bazaar in the Painter's Ward that sells high-level illusion scrolls, exotic, magically-flavoured wines, and enchanted luxury textiles.
- The Grand Amphitheatre of Eidas: A massive, open-air theatre dedicated to [[Eidas, God Of Art]]. Rather than silent prayer, worship here consists of grand, theatrical duels and dramatic performances that last for days.

# Key NPCs

- [[Grand Duke Valerius the Blind]]: (Level 16 Human Aristocrat) The ruler of the city. Decades ago, he magically blinded himself so he would never accidentally see the un-illusioned world again if the Veil were to flicker.
- Maestra Vanya: (Level 14 Fetchling Illusionist) The cynical, overworked head of the Veil-Weavers. She knows exactly how fragile the city's defences truly are, as she is the one desperately keeping the magic running.

# Factions & Guilds

- The Veil-Weavers: The elite guild of bards, illusionists, and Occult casters whose full-time job is maintaining the city-wide mirages. They hold immense secret power.
- The Twilight Court: The dominant political faction of the aristocracy, constantly vying for the Duke's favour through artistic patronage and lethal gossip.

# History & Lore

Before the [[War of Laws]], Prismathia was the genuine cultural and artistic capital of Cenora. When the cataclysm struck and the horrific Daeshin Null-State formed on their immediate border, the city's mages realised they lacked the martial strength to fight the reality-warped monsters. Instead, the artists painted over the apocalypse. For 116 years, they have poured all their magical and economic resources into maintaining the Great Masquerade, choosing opulent denial over fighting for a better future.
