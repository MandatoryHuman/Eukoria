# Overview

Located in the northeast of [[Cenora]], The Radiant Court of Gethis is a nation in deep, opulent denial. Geographically located just next to the reality-warping madness of [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Daeshin Null-State/index|The Daeshin Null-State]] to the south to the North, Gethis should be a war-torn hellscape, much like [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Glandaric Marches/index|The Glandaric Marches]]. Instead, the aristocracy has utilised staggering amounts of Illusion and Occult magic to literally cloak their nation in an eternal, perfect twilight masquerade.

# Geography & Climate

The actual terrain of Gethis is bleak, rocky, and heavily scarred by the proximity to Daeshin. However, very few citizens ever see the true sky. The major cities and noble estates are covered by massive, state-sponsored mirages. To walk into a Gethian city is to step out of a grey wasteland and into a vibrant, endless sunset festival, filled with impossible bioluminescence, phantom architecture, and artificially sweetened air.

# Society & Culture

Gethian society is the pinnacle of decadence, high art, and political backstabbing. They revere [[Eidas, God Of Art]] and [[Lethos, God of Trickery]], viewing truth as something entirely malleable. Physical labour is done by paid foreigners or enchanted automatons, while the citizens spend their lives attending endless galas, artistic exhibitions, and theatrical duels.

However, this opulent lifestyle is a trauma response to the apocalypse on their borders. It is considered a massive social faux pas - sometimes even a punishable crime - to acknowledge the reality storms or the crumbling state of the world outside the illusions. If a monster from the Null-State breaches the border, the nobles simply hire mercenaries to quietly dispose of it while the orchestra plays louder.

# Government & Politics

Gethis is ruled by an incredibly vain aristocracy led by Grand Duke Valerius, who literally blinded himself so he would never have to see the un-illusioned world again. Politics is entirely court-based intrigue. Poison in a wine glass, magical slander, and framing rivals for treason are common daily occurrences.

# Diplomatic Relations

- [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Glandaric Marches/index|The Glandaric Marches]] (Disgust/Pity): The scarred, traumatised soldiers of the Marches utterly loathe the Gethians, viewing them as cowardly, powdered fops ignoring the real war. Gethis views the Marches as depressing brutes who lack aesthetic taste.
- [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Directorate of Vael/index|The Directorate of Vael]] (Hostile): Vael’s obsession with absolute truth and transparency puts them at fundamental ideological odds with Gethis's illusionary society, frequently resulting in espionage and diplomatic sabotage.

# History & Lore

Before the [[War of Laws]], Gethis was the cultural and artistic capital of Cenora. When the cataclysm struck and the Daeshin Null-State formed on their border, the Gethian mages realised they lacked the martial strength of the Glandaric Marches or the psychic discipline of Vael to fight it. Instead, the artists painted over the apocalypse. For 116 years, they have poured all their magical resources into maintaining the "Great Masquerade", pretending the world never ended.

# Notable Locations

- [[Prismathia]]: The capital city, a dizzying metropolis of glass, crystal, and hard-light illusions where the architecture changes daily based on the current artistic trends of the ruling Duke.
- The Painted Border: The southern border where Gethis meets [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Daeshin Null-State/index|Daeshin]]. It looks like a beautiful, serene painting of a mountain range from a distance, but stepping through the illusion reveals the horrific, ash-choked reality of the Null-State.

# Prominent Factions

- The Veil-Weavers: The elite guild of bards, illusionists, and Occult casters whose full-time job is maintaining the city-wide mirages. They hold immense secret power, as they can "drop the veil" over a rival's estate, exposing them to the raw horrors of reality.
