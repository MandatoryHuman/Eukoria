# Overview

Seiran is a misty, mountainous nation defined by an unending relationship with the supernatural. It is an Asian-inspired shogunate drawn from Japanese aesthetics and feudal structure, where the veil between the material plane and the spirit world is thin enough to be crossed by ritual alone.

# Geography & Climate

Seiran's forests are full of territorial kami, its towns are haunted by mischievous or malevolent yokai, and its coasts are alive with restless drowned spirits. Villagers do not simply cut timber or build roads: they perform rites, leave offerings, and maintain protective wards before any work begins. The land itself is treated as a living host that must be appeased.

# Society & Culture

Seiranese society is deeply ritualistic and observant. Architecture is designed to confuse vengeful ghosts, while families maintain offerings, talismans, and ancestral shrines to keep the spirits from wandering into daily life. Spirit-mediums, onmyoji, and exorcists serve as both priests and strategists, shaping the nation's military, government, and even its most mundane civic functions.

# Government & Politics

The military and the state are inseparable from the spiritual world. Seiran's magistrates, wardens, and naval officers work in concert with mediums and onmyoji who deploy ofuda, spirit-binding rites, and protective arrays alongside steel, black powder, and warships. Governing requires understanding both the living and the dead.

# Diplomatic Relations

The most dangerous frontier in Seiran is the western maritime margin. The Far Prefectures of Ryoshi and Tsuru sit in the strategic waterways between Antora and Cenora, where Seiran maintains a tense, decades-long cold war against Meridia. Open war is avoided by mutual assured destruction, but espionage, blockade running, assassinations, and naval skirmishes continue beneath a constant climate of dread.

# History & Lore

The spirit-haunted infrastructure of Seiran was shaped by the [[War of Laws]], but the state was also forged by the ongoing shadow conflict between the Far Prefectures and Meridia. The warding arrays along the cliffs project magical fields that disrupt Meridian scrying, while Seirani shinobi and Meridian privateers silently clash in the fog of the straits. Seiran's identity is built as much around control and concealment as it is around reverence.

# Notable Locations

- [[Kyouten]]: The capital and ceremonial heart of the shogunate, wrapped in mists and spiritual defence.
- The Far Prefectures: Ryoshi and Tsuru, twin fortified islands bristling with artillery, wards, and spy-craft.
- The Antora-Cenora divide: The strategic strait where Seiran and Meridia contest the maritime balance through shadow war.

# Prominent Factions

- The onmyoji and spirit-mediums: Ritual specialists who bind, ward, and guide supernatural forces in service of the state.
- The prefectural magistrates: Civil and military authorities responsible for law, logistics, and the upkeep of Seiran's defensive rites.
- The Far Prefecture commandants: Officers tasked with holding the island fortresses against Meridian pressure.
