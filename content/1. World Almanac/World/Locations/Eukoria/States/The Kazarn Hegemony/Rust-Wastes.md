# Overview

The Rust-Wastes are a region of horrific monstrosities where supernatural entropy continues to eat away at habitable land. Travellers measure a journey by the condition of their equipment rather than by distance: a road that takes a day in clear weather may consume a week of provisions when an entropic squall forces the caravan underground.

# Ecology & Environment

Grey ash plains, rust-red ravines, and isolated pockets of tough, metallic grass define the Wastes. Entropic squalls can arrive without a change in wind or temperature, so caravans travel by relay between stone shelters and carry duplicate tools sealed in waxed leather. Food is preserved as hardtack, smoked root vegetables, salt-cured meat, and concentrated fruit paste; fresh water is stored in ceramic or glass because exposed metal taints it within hours.

Travellers follow strict etiquette around the Wastes. A caravan approaching a shelter announces its headcount, destination, and the condition of its supplies before crossing the threshold. No one touches another traveller's pack, blade, or water vessel without permission, and every group leaves one sealed ration behind for the next party. Breaking these customs marks a traveller as a rust-thief, and few guides will share a road with one.

# Hazards & Encounters

The common dangers are entropic squalls, sinkholes opening beneath ash crusts, and creatures whose bodies have adapted to accelerated decay.

# Natural Resources

The Wastes yield Kazarni iron, storm-glass, ash-cured fungal medicines, and the rare metal-eating moss used to make corrosion-resistant tools. Every resource is costly to harvest: a mine may be rich in ore but reachable only during the brief intervals between squalls.

# Myths & Lore

Kazarn guides say the first shelters were built from the ribs of a sleeping iron giant, and that the Rust-Wastes expand whenever someone takes more than they need. The story is repeated to children as a warning against waste, but prospectors still search for the buried giant beneath the ash.
