# Overview

Soma-Nadir is the capital of [[The Somatic Sanctuary of Vane]], a haven of bodily autonomy and clinical biokinesis. The city is nested inside the colossal, bleached ribcage of an ancient reality-warped leviathan that washed ashore centuries ago.

# Geography & Layout

The leviathan's bones have been carved into sterile surgical theatres, recovery cloisters, and flesh-sculpting salons. These spaces hang from pulsing, engineered connective tissue rather than ropes or chains, creating a living architectural network within the ribcage.

# Government & Law

The hospital-cloisters of [[Lysia Bind-breaker, Goddess of Passion|Lysia Bind-breaker]] govern the city. Their authority rests on the protection of patients, practitioners, and Vane's right to practice radical anatomical modification openly.

# Districts

- The Surgical Theatres: Sterile facilities carved into the leviathan's ribs.
- The Recovery Cloisters: Healing quarters suspended in engineered connective tissue.
- The Flesh-Sculpting Salons: Public studios where Fleshwarpers and Passion-Mages practice bodily transformation.

# Notable Locations

- The leviathan's ribcage: Soma-Nadir's bleached, reality-warped foundation.
- The surgical theatres: Clinical centres for advanced fleshcraft.
- The connective-tissue spans: Pulsing supports that suspend the city's districts.

# Key NPCs

- The cloister surgeons: Practitioners who maintain the city's medical institutions.
- The Passion-Mages: Fleshcraft specialists combining magic with clinical precision.

# Factions & Guilds

- The hospital-cloisters of Lysia Bind-breaker: Soma-Nadir's ruling institutions.
- The Fleshwarpers: Surgeons who treat the body as a canvas of self-expression under [[Lysia Bind-breaker, Goddess of Passion|Lysia Bind-breaker]].
- The Passion-Mages: Magical practitioners of anatomical modification.

# History & Lore

Soma-Nadir grew within the remains of a leviathan that had been warped by reality before washing ashore. Vanean surgeons transformed the bones into a functioning sanctuary, turning an unsettling relic into a symbol of bodily freedom and defiance toward [[The Tralichuan Expanse|the Tralichuan]] armadas.
