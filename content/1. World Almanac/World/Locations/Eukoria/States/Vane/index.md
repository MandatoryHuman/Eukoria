# Overview

The Somatic Sanctuary of Vane is a refuge for Fleshwarps and anyone seeking radical anatomical modification. It lies in south-west [[Cenora]] between [[The Tralichuan Expanse]], which violently rejects biological artificiality, and the rigidly skeletal [[The Jhalian Necrocracy]].

# Geography & Climate

Vane occupies a tense south-western frontier between hostile neighbours. Its settlements are built around clinical facilities and protected supply routes, while [[The Tralichuan Expanse|Tralichuan]] naval patrols threaten the coast with crusades against Vanean fleshcraft.

# Society & Culture

Vane treats the body as a canvas of self-expression. Brilliant surgeons and Passion-Mages use clinical precision to alter flesh, giving Fleshwarps and other seekers a place to escape the rigid expectations of neighbouring societies. Bodily autonomy is a civic principle as well as a medical practice.

# Government & Politics

The sanctuary is governed by the hospital-cloisters of [[Lysia Bind-breaker, Goddess of Passion|Lysia Bind-breaker]]. Authority rests with the surgeons, Fleshwarpers, and Passion-Mages capable of maintaining the cloisters and defending Vane's right to practice anatomical modification.

# Diplomatic Relations

Vane is opposed by [[The Tralichuan Expanse]], whose biological orthodoxy makes the sanctuary a prime target for naval crusades. Its position beside [[The Jhalian Necrocracy]] creates a second ideological pressure, as Vane's living, self-directed fleshcraft stands against Jhal's skeletal rigidity.

# History & Lore

Vane grew into a sanctuary as surgeons and Fleshwarpers gathered to practice openly beyond the reach of hostile biological orthodoxy. Under the hospital-cloisters of [[Lysia Bind-breaker, Goddess of Passion|Lysia Bind-breaker]], clinical biokinesis became both a craft and a declaration of personal freedom.

# Notable Locations

- [[Soma-Nadir]]: The capital, built within the ribcage of an ancient reality-warped leviathan.
- The Tralichuan blockade: A dangerous naval cordon threatening Vanean supplies and independence.
- The hospital-cloisters: Surgical and spiritual institutions where fleshcraft is taught and practiced.

# Prominent Factions

- The hospital-cloisters of Lysia Bind-breaker: Vane's ruling institutions.
- The Fleshwarpers: Surgeons who perform radical anatomical modification.
- The Passion-Mages: Practitioners who combine magic and clinical precision to reshape living bodies.
