# Overview

Tōrō is the capital of [[The Prefecture of Seiran]], a breathtaking coastal metropolis suspended over a perpetually misty bay. Its floating wooden piers and paper-lantern-lit barges form a massive tactical puzzle designed to confuse the Funa-yūrei haunting the waters.

# Geography & Layout

The city is built across a network of floating piers and barges. At dusk, harbour-masters rotate key bridges and shift floating wards to sever spiritual ley-lines, purposefully changing the route inland and breaking the ghosts' linear paths in service of [[Amnis the Burdened, Goddess of Forgiveness|Amnis]].

# Government & Law

Harbour-masters coordinate the city's shifting infrastructure and spiritual defenses. The city's laws and routes are inseparable from ward maintenance: a solved or broken ward can flood whole villages with drowned apparitions.

# Districts

- The Lantern Harbour: Paper-lantern-lit barges and the city's main maritime approaches.
- The Rotating Bridges: Movable crossings used to reshape the city's spiritual maze.
- The Mistward: Floating wards and investigation routes over the haunted bay.

# Notable Locations

- The floating ward network: A shifting system of spiritual defenses.
- The misty bay: Haunted by Funa-yūrei formed from the [[War of Laws]]' tidal waves.
- The harbour-master's routes: The changing bridges used to sever spiritual ley-lines.

# Key NPCs

- The harbour-masters: Officials responsible for rotating bridges and shifting wards.
- The emissaries of [[Amnis the Burdened, Goddess of Forgiveness|Amnis]]: Ghost investigators who guide the drowned back to the depths.

# Factions & Guilds

- The harbour-masters: Tōrō's logistical and defensive authority.
- The emissaries of Amnis: Pacifistic exorcists and spiritual guides.

# History & Lore

Tōrō was engineered as a response to the [[War of Laws]] and the restless dead it left along Seiran's coast. Every bridge, barge, and ward contributes to the city's central strategy: confuse the drowned without destroying them.
