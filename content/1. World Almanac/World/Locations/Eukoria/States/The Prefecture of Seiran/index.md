# Overview

The Prefecture of Seiran lies on the southern coast of [[Antoria]] and bears the spiritual runoff of the [[War of Laws]]. Its coastline is haunted by vengeful, water-bound spirits, so Seiran's communities survive through ritual, communion, and architecture designed to confuse the dead.

# Geography & Climate

Seiran is a misty coastal nation of bays, floating piers, and shifting waterways. Restless spirits formed by catastrophic tidal waves during the [[War of Laws]] haunt the coast, turning otherwise familiar routes into dangerous spiritual corridors.

# Society & Culture

Seiranese society is deeply ritualistic. Architecture incorporates elaborate logic puzzles and labyrinthine wards that trap or confuse linear-thinking ghosts. Ghost investigators and emissaries of [[Amnis the Burdened, Goddess of Forgiveness|Amnis]] navigate these structures nightly, guiding drowned spirits back to the depths through pacifistic exorcism.

# Government & Politics

Authority is organized around the maintenance of spiritual wards and the safe movement of coastal communities. Harbour-masters control the city's floating infrastructure and rotate bridges and wards to sever spiritual ley-lines when the ghosts begin to wander inland.

# Diplomatic Relations

Seiran's greatest concern is the containment of the War of Laws' spiritual aftermath. Its coastal settlements depend on warding expertise and peaceful communion with the dead, and any saboteur who opens a spirit-ward threatens the entire prefecture.

# History & Lore

The [[War of Laws]] left Seiran with a coastline full of drowned dead. Rather than destroy the spirits, the followers of [[Amnis the Burdened, Goddess of Forgiveness|Amnis]] developed a tradition of forgiveness, investigation, and carefully engineered confusion. This practice shaped both the nation's religion and its cities.

# Notable Locations

- [[Tōrō]]: The capital, a floating labyrinth of piers, barges, bridges, and shifting wards.
- The coastal spirit-wards: Protective puzzle-structures that keep drowned apparitions from reaching inland villages.
- The haunted southern coast: The source of Seiran's restless Funa-yūrei.

# Prominent Factions

- The harbour-masters: Custodians of the floating routes and the city's shifting ward network.
- Emissaries of Amnis: Pacifistic ghost investigators who commune with and guide the drowned.
- The ward-keepers: Ritual specialists responsible for maintaining Seiran's logic puzzles and labyrinthine defenses.
