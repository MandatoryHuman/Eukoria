# Overview

Once a disparate collection of northern territories, Fraedal has been forged into the Fraedalan Synchrony: a deeply secretive, unorthodox, and heavily surveilled state. Inspired by a blend of Tsarist mystique and brutalist bureaucracy, the Synchrony embraces a state religion built around [[Aporia, God of Fate]], the taboo god of destiny and paradox.

# Geography & Climate

The freezing taiga of Fraedal is dotted with massive, ticking geometric obelisks and strange, brutalist monuments that anchor local timelines. The land is harsh, the weather punishing, and the environment itself is shaped by temporal anomalies. Whole districts can slip between states of reality as gears turn beneath the earth and the stars align in impossible ways.

# Society & Culture

Fraedalan culture is defined by fatalism, surveillance, and probability. Its scholars, artificers, and seers-the Apparatchiks of the Loom-believe the world is already unfolding along a single mathematically inevitable path. To survive the coming cataclysm, the Synchrony studies every possible divergence and tries to align itself with the one perfect alternate timeline where reality does not collapse again.

# Government & Politics

The state is a machine of bureaucracy, ritual, and temporal control. Fate-Weavers sit in frozen meditation at the Zenith Observatory and chart the kingdom's future with ruthless intent, while chronal engineers maintain the obelisks and district-shifting gears that keep the Synchrony stable in the face of destabilized causality. The citizenry is watched constantly, and dissent is treated as a dangerous deviation from the timeline.

# Diplomatic Relations

Fraedal remains inward-looking and deeply suspicious of the outside world. Its scholars understand the danger of the future more than they trust any neighbour. The Synchrony does not seek conquest so much as inevitability: it believes catastrophe is mathematically unavoidable, and it aims to survive the collapse by becoming the one power best prepared to endure it.

# History & Lore

The Synchrony's central dogma is the Mathematical Inevitability: another cataclysm on the scale of the [[War of Laws]] is mathematically inevitable. To prepare, its engineers use orthodox Arcane magic and localised temporal anomalies to gaze into, and align with, the one perfect alternate timeline. The process is terrifying, expensive, and impossible to fully understand, yet it has become the foundation of the entire state.

# Notable Locations

- [[Ephemera]]: The clockwork capital of the Synchrony, a city that phases between timelines as magnetic gears and subterranean observatories turn.
- The ticking obelisks: Brutalist monuments that anchor local time and help the state chart future divergences.
- The Zenith Observatory: The vast gear-shaped citadel where the Fate-Weavers watch the kingdom's destiny unfold.

# Prominent Factions

- The Apparatchiks of the Loom: Scholars, seers, and engineers who study the threads of probability and inevitability.
- The Fate-Weavers: Ruling mystics whose meditations determine which lines of history the state will pursue.
- The chronal engineers: Artificers who maintain the gears, districts, and time-wards that keep Ephemera and the surrounding taiga stable.
