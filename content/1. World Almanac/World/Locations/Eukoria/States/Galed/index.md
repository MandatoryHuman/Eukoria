# Overview

Galed, also called the Rime-Hold, is a punishing ice-locked stratocracy where survival is the only true measure of worth. Endless blizzards and supernatural cold have turned the region into a monument to mortal endurance.

# Geography & Climate

Galed is battered by blizzards and a supernatural cold that leeches Life essence from unprotected travellers. Frozen lakes, scarce heat sources, and deep glacier crevasses make the environment an active tactical hazard. The climate was permanently shattered by the clash between [[Vellora, Goddess of Sea and Storms]] and the void of [[Kenon, The Absence]] during the celestial rebellion.

# Society & Culture

Galed is brutally pragmatic and meritocratic. Humans, thick-furred beastkin, and towering orcs measure wealth in heavy furs, preserved meat, and forged iron. Weakness is treated as a luxury, and the populace reveres [[Bella, Goddess of War]]. Galed views [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/Meridia/index|Meridia]]'s industrial magic as soft and decadent.

# Government & Politics

The Rime-Hold is ruled by warlords whose authority is proven through endurance and command. The ruling council meets at the exposed High Seat so that their physical Spirit and Presence remain visibly unbroken by [[Vellora, Goddess of Sea and Storms|Vellora]]'s storms.

# Diplomatic Relations

Galed is suspicious of softer, more industrialized powers such as [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/Meridia/index|Meridia]]. Its greatest external threats are the supernatural cold, the predators hidden by blizzards, and necromancers of [[Aethelgard, God of Relics|Aethelgard]] who seek the perfectly preserved bodies stored beneath Skorvall.

# History & Lore

During the celestial rebellion, [[Vellora, Goddess of Sea and Storms|Vellora]]'s clash with the void of [[Kenon, The Absence]] shattered Galed's climate. The resulting ambient magic leeches Life from the exposed, forcing generations of people to rely on the raw physical resilience of Matter. The Rime-Hold's culture of endurance grew from that catastrophe.

# Notable Locations

- [[Skorvall]]: The glacier capital, carved into the heart of a colossal, slow-moving glacier.
- The High Seat: The exposed command fortress where the warlords hold council.
- The Howling Ward: The glacier's deepest district, storing emergency rations and preserved bodies.

# Prominent Factions

- The warlords of the High Seat: Galed's ruling military elite.
- The orthodox clerics of the Howling Ward: Guardians of the preserved dead and watchers against Aethelgard's necromancers.
- Galed's beastkin and orc holds: Hardened communities that supply soldiers, hunters, furs, meat, and iron.
