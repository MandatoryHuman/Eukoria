# Overview

Valerius is the gleaming capital of Hokiel, a city of towering white-stone keeps and ceremonial avenues. It is the foremost expression of the kingdom's chivalric ideals, where noble pageantry, orthodox faith, and military readiness shape daily life.

# Geography & Layout

The city stands amid Hokiel's fertile North-Eastern plains, with broad roads designed for cavalry processions and the movement of armoured hosts. White-stone fortifications and high keeps dominate the skyline, while the Grand Cathedral of the Honoured Spirit forms the spiritual heart of the capital.

# Government & Law

Valerius is ruled directly by King Oric the Resolute and serves as the seat of the Parliament of Dukes. Bella's clerics oversee trials by combat and honourable duels, while the city's laws are closely tied to orthodox faith and the kingdom's prohibition of Dismagic.

# Districts

- The Royal Keep: The king's fortified residence and the meeting place of the Parliament of Dukes.
- The Cathedral Ward: A processional district surrounding the Grand Cathedral of the Honoured Spirit and the sanctuaries of Amnis's clerics.
- The White-Stone Markets: The commercial heart of the capital, supplied by Hokiel's grain, horse, armour, and scroll trades.

# Notable Locations

- Grand Cathedral of the Honoured Spirit: The capital's foremost temple, dedicated to the orthodox union of Life and Spirit.
- Parliament of Dukes: The seat of the highborn nobles who advise the absolute monarch.
- Soul-Testing Sanctuaries: Religious halls where citizens may submit to Divine Magic scanning to demonstrate that their Spirit remains untainted by cosmic friction.

# Key NPCs

- King Oric the Resolute: The absolute monarch of Hokiel.
- The Cathedral's High Cleric: The senior religious authority overseeing the capital's rites and trials under Bella's watch.

# Factions & Guilds

- The Radiant Vanguard: Elite paladins of Bella who serve as the king's personal enforcers and battlefield commanders.
- The Sin-Eater Envoys: Clerics of Amnis who mediate disputes between rival noble houses.
- Parliament of Dukes: The kingdom's assembly of highborn nobles.

# History & Lore

Valerius grew into the seat of Hokiel's rulers after the coalition of paladins who survived the [[War of Laws]] anchored their souls to the North-Eastern plains. Its keeps and cathedral preserve the memory of that divine protection and the descendants who inherited it.
