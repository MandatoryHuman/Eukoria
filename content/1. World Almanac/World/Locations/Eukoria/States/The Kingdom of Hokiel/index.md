# Overview

Nestled in the North-Eastern plains of [[Antora]], the Kingdom of Hokiel is a proud and highly traditional feudal monarchy. To the rest of the continent, it is the romanticised, chivalric shield of the north. Where its southern neighbours in the [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Kazarn Hegemony/index|The Kazarn Hegemony]] rely on a grim, utilitarian stratocracy to survive, Hokiel embraces glorious pageantry, divine right, and the orthodox purity of Divine Magic: the harmonious blend of Life and Spirit.

# Geography & Climate

The kingdom is defined by rolling, fertile plains that give way to alpine foothills in the north. It is bordered by the [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Scholasticate of Khalen/index|The Scholasticate of Khalen]] to the east, the [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn/index|The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn]] to the west, and the heavily fortified borders of the [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Kazarn Hegemony/index|The Kazarn Hegemony]] to the south. The climate is temperate but prone to harsh, biting winters, forcing the populace to rely on the fortified warmth of grand, stone-walled cities and bountiful summer harvests.

# Society & Culture

Hokielan society is rigidly stratified, built upon a classic feudal system of serfs, merchants, and highborn nobles. This hierarchy is entirely underpinned by orthodox faith. The populace deeply reveres [[Bella, Goddess of War]], viewing honourable, face-to-face combat as the ultimate test of a mortal's Spirit. Knights of Hokiel are expected to act as literal beacons of morale; cowardly tactics, subterfuge, and the use of poison are culturally abhorrent.

The presence of [[Amnis the Burdened, Goddess of Forgiveness]] is also heavily felt. Her pacifist clerics operate sprawling sanctuaries that serve as neutral ground, mediating disputes between rival noble houses. The use of [[Traditions of Dismagic|Dismagic]] is strictly outlawed and viewed with intense disgust, leading to the culturally accepted practice of Soul-Testing: a ritual where citizens voluntarily submit to Divine Magic scanning to prove their Spirit remains untainted by cosmic friction.

# Government & Politics

The kingdom is ruled by an absolute monarch, currently King Oric the Resolute, supported by the Parliament of Dukes. Justice is frequently settled through trial by combat or honourable duels under the watchful eyes of Bella's clerics.

The military is heavily cavalry-focused, boasting orders of paladins and divine spellcasters who ride alongside heavily armoured knights.

# Diplomatic Relations

- [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Kazarn Hegemony/index|The Kazarn Hegemony]] (Tense Allies): While they share a border and a patron deity in Bella, Hokiel views the Kazarni as grim zealots devoid of nobility. Kazarn, in turn, views Hokiel's pageantry as an inefficient waste of resources. They unite only when the reality-warping threats of the Rust-Wastes spill too far north.
- [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Scholasticate of Khalen/index|The Scholasticate of Khalen]] (Strong Allies): Hokiel relies on Khalen for Arcane and Occult infrastructure, trading its agricultural surplus and mercenary protection for magical wards.
- [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn/index|The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn]] (Hostile): Frequent, violent skirmishes occur on the western border, where Hokiel's knights launch crusades to bring order to the untamed lands.

# History & Lore

The kingdom was forged in the aftermath of the [[War of Laws]] in 0 AW. While the oceans boiled and mountains were ground into glass, a coalition of paladins anchored their souls to the land using pure orthodox faith, shielding the North-Eastern plains from the worst of the divine collateral damage. Their descendants now rule from those very keeps.

# Notable Locations

- [[Valerius]]: The gleaming capital city, famous for its towering white-stone keeps and the Grand Cathedral of the Honoured Spirit.
- [[The Argent Marches]]: The heavily patrolled western borderland separating Hokiel from Oremourn, dotted with crusader castles.

# Prominent Factions

- The Radiant Vanguard: Elite paladins of Bella who serve as the King's personal enforcers and battlefield commanders.
- The Sin-Eater Envoys: Diplomatic clerics of Amnis who travel between feuding fiefdoms, absorbing the sins of remorseful lords to maintain the kingdom's internal peace.
