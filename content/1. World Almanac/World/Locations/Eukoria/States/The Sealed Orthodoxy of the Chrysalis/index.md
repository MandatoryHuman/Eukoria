# Overview

The Sealed Orthodoxy of the Chrysalis is a sprawling, rigidly bureaucratic theocracy located in northern [[Antoria]]. Surrounded by the entropic decay of [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Kazarn Hegemony/index|Kazarn]], the wild storms of the [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Rastorian Reach/index|Rastorian Reach]], and the volatile magitek of [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn/index|Oremourn]], the Chrysalis survived the [[War of Laws]] through an extreme act of magical isolation; spinning a literal, state-spanning cocoon of Abjuration magic over their borders.

Today, they are a hyper-organized hegemony that views themselves as the final bastion of cosmic order, waiting in absolute quarantine for the "diseased" world outside their shell to simply die off. They are feared for their "Mage-Hounds" - inquisitors specialized in counter-magic who aggressively ensure this internal purity.

# Geography & Climate

The Chrysalis occupies a heavily fortified, elevated plateau. The climate is unnaturally stable, a direct result of the colossal "Aegis-Towers" that border the nation. These towers project an invisible, shimmering dome of Abjuration magic that repels the volatile reality storms, magical fallout, and freezing northern weather. The natural borders are steep, craggy mountain ranges that have been smoothed and carved into immense, geometric fortifications by arcane transmuters to perfectly anchor the magical shell above.

# Society & Culture

Daily life in the Chrysalis is governed by the "Sanctioned Path," a massive legal and religious codex dictating everything from trade to acceptable magical practices. The state operates as an ideological prison; privacy is practically non-existent, as citizens are encouraged to report any signs of anomalous magic or emotional irregularity (which they believe leads to Dismagic). Despite the rigid laws, the citizens enjoy a high standard of living, free from the starvation or mutant horrors found in the rest of Antoria. Ancestries that lean towards order thrive in the bureaucratic hierarchy, while chaotic or innately magical beings are treated with extreme suspicion.

# Government & Politics

The state operates as a Theocratic Bureaucracy. The [[Hierophant|Grand Hierophant Odanis]] sits at the apex, but the true power lies within the thousands of interlocking ministries that manage the nation. Magic is heavily policed by the Ministry of the Aegis. To cast a spell above the simplest cantrips without a state-issued "Caster's Writ" is a severe crime. Justice is swift, cold, and entirely devoid of emotion, often resulting in the offender having their magical connection permanently severed.

# Diplomatic Relations

- [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn/index|The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn]] (Hostile): The strict abjurers of the Chrysalis view the volatile scrap-tech and localized Dismagic of their western neighbour as an apocalyptic threat, frequently attempting to blockade or sabotage them to prevent their "rot" from spreading.
- [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Kazarn Hegemony/index|The Kazarn Hegemony]] (Tense Cold War): While both are highly militarized and value order, the Chrysalis finds Kazarn's philosophical acceptance of entropy to be deeply heretical. They heavily garrison their southern border to prevent Kazarn's Rust-Wastes from creeping towards their pristine walls.
- [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Scholasticate of Khalen/index|The Scholasticate of Khalen]] (Trade Partners): The Chrysalis respects Khalen's orthodox preservation of history, frequently trading elite Mage-Hound protection for copies of recovered pre-war magical theory.

# History & Lore

During the climax of the [[War of Laws]], when the fabric of [[Fundamental Essences|Matter]] was being torn apart across Antoria, a coalition of high-priests and abjurers performed the "Great Sealing." They sacrificed thousands of willing citizens to power an array of monolithic wards, physically severing their lands from the worst of the magical fallout. They took the name "The Chrysalis" to represent their metamorphosis: shedding the vulnerable chaos of the old world to incubate a perfectly ordered, impenetrable future.

# Notable Locations

- **Aegis-Gath:** The capital city, constructed entirely from white, magically-reinforced marble. It is built in concentric circles, with the Ministry of the Aegis at its very centre.
- **The Obsidian Penitent:** A massive, magically nullified prison carved into a canyon on the Oremourn border. It is where unsanctioned casters, rogue alchemists, and practitioners of Dismagic are sent to have their minds "purified."

# Prominent Factions

- **The Order of the Mage-Hounds:** Elite inquisitors trained specifically in Counterspell and anti-magic tactics. They are granted special authority to bypass standard bureaucracy to hunt rogue mages.
- **The Ministry of the Aegis:** The bureaucratic arm responsible for maintaining the warding towers that protect the state's borders.
