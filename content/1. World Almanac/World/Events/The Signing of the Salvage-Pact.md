# Overview
Occurring exactly thirty years ago, the Signing of the Salvage-Pact brought an end to a brutal, decades-long civil war between the various warlords of northern [[Antoria]][cite: 3]. By formally uniting the disparate scavenger clans, it established the chaotic but functional coalition known today as [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn/index|The Salvage-Pact of Oremourn]][cite: 3].

# Prelude & Causes
For decades following the [[War of Laws]], the irradiated, scrap-choked wastes of northern Antoria were violently contested by rival warlords and rogue inventors[cite: 3]. The conflict reached its boiling point when a colossal, un-looted celestial dreadnought was uncovered beneath the ash[cite: 3]. The resulting turf war over its celestial metals and latent magitek sparked a massive, multi-faction vehicular bloodbath that threatened to completely wipe out the surviving population[cite: 3].

# The Event
After months of mechanised trench warfare, a stalemate was reached. Realising that continued fighting would only leave them vulnerable to the encroaching forces of [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Kazarn Hegemony/index|The Kazarn Hegemony]], the most powerful Scrap-Barons called a parley[cite: 3]. Meeting within the hollowed-out skull of the fallen celestial war-machine, they drafted a blood-and-oil treaty[cite: 3]. 

The Pact mandated that the dreadnought would not belong to a single Baron, but would instead serve as a neutral capital[cite: 3]. Furthermore, it required all Barons to cease internal hostilities whenever a foreign incursion threatened the wastes[cite: 3].

# Aftermath & Legacy
The signing successfully unified the wastes[cite: 3]. The dreadnought was converted into the sprawling shanty-metropolis known as The Great Hulk[cite: 3]. The treaty also established the Council of Iron, which continues to meet annually to negotiate trade and settle disputes through ritualised vehicular combat rather than all-out war[cite: 3]. The unified front allowed Oremourn to become a formidable, independent state built entirely on salvaged technology and volatile black powder[cite: 3].
