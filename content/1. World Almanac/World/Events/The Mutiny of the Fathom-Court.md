# Overview

The Mutiny of the Fathom-Court was a violent naval coup that extinguished the last remnants of aristocratic rule on the eastern coast of [[Cenora]]. It resulted in the formation of the Lash-Bound Syndicate of the Fathom-Court, transitioning the region from a struggling kingdom into a massive, lawless maritime alliance.

# Prelude & Causes

As the overland trade routes collapsed and the [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/States/The Daeshin Null-State/index|Daeshin Null-State]] expanded, eastern coastal survivors fled to the water. The original royal family of the region attempted to maintain their strict taxation and aristocratic hierarchy over these desperate, floating refugee camps. As starvation set in and the royals hoarded resources aboard their heavily armed galleons, unrest grew among the smugglers, pirates, and magical navigators who were doing the actual work to keep the flotilla afloat.

# The Event

The uprising was swift and brutal. Led by the infamous Pirate King [[Malachi The Lash]], a coalition of smugglers coordinated a simultaneous sabotage of the royal fleet's rudders. Immobilised, the royal flagship was quickly boarded by Malachi's crew. 

In a highly theatrical display, Malachi executed the aristocracy and sank their gold-laden galleons. He then retrieved the drowned king's royal land charter from the wreckage, nailed it to the mainmast of his own ship, and sarcastically declared his lashed-together raft of pirate vessels the "Fathom-Court".

# Aftermath & Legacy

The mutiny completely reorganised eastern coastal society. The old royal decrees were replaced by the "Favour Economy," establishing a meritocracy based entirely on maritime skill and survival. The term "Fathom-Court" stuck, eventually becoming the namesake for the sprawling, semi-mobile naval syndicate that now controls the illicit oceanic trade of [[1. World Almanac/World/Locations/Eukoria/index|Eukoria]].
